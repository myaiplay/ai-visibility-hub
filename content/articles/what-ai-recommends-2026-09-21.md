# What AI search recommended this week

<p class="meta">21 September 2026 · Based on 119 recorded test runs · AI Visibility Index</p>

AI search engines are already acting like recommendation layers for local services, and the names they surface are fairly consistent within each city and query phrasing. In these recorded test runs, a small set of businesses dominated each category, with top names appearing in a majority of answers.

## What the data shows

For **house cleaner in Denver, CO**, the most frequently named business was **Alpine Maids**, which appeared in **100%** of answers across **20** test runs. The next most visible names were **MaidPro Denver** at **80%**, **Merit Maids** at **65%**, and both **Superb Maids Denver** and **The Maids** at **50%**.

A small wording change shifted the pattern. For **house cleaner in Denver, CO?** the leader changed to **MaidPro Denver** at **95%** of **20** test runs, while **Alpine Maids** fell to **80%**. **The Maids** rose to **75%**, **Merit Maids** to **70%**, and **Kathy Clean** appeared in **35%** of answers.

That same sensitivity shows up in Australia. For **landscaper in Brisbane, Australia**, **Designer Image** was named in **75%** of answers across **20** test runs, followed by **Human Nature Landscapes** at **70%**, **Logan Lawn Mowing & Garden Care** at **55%**, **BGL Services** at **45%**, and **Mow on lawn solutions** at **40%**. But for **landscaper in Brisbane, Australia?**, **Aboveground Landscapes** and **Human Nature Landscapes** were tied at **80%**, with **Martin Brothers** at **60%**, **Crivello Landscapes** at **50%**, and **Brizscapes** at **45%**.

Sydney showed a different cluster again. For **landscaper in Sydney, Australia?**, **NBG Landscapes** led at **85%** of **20** test runs, while **Dr Garden** reached **70%**. **Concept Landscaping**, **Love Landscapes**, and **Sydney Landscaping Pty Ltd** each appeared in **55%** of answers.

Austin’s cleaning market was also highly concentrated. For **house cleaner in Austin, TX**, **Eleven Friends of Grumpy Brush** was named in **95%** of **19** test runs, and **Sparkly Maid Austin Cleaning Services** followed closely at **89%**. **Tidy Hands Cleaners** appeared in **63%** of answers, **Maids and Moore** in **53%**, and **Mystik Cleaning Service** in **26%**.

## What stands out across the tests

The strongest pattern is concentration: AI search engines repeatedly recommend a short list rather than spreading attention evenly across many local businesses. In every query set here, the top two or three names captured most of the visible recommendations.

Another clear pattern is query phrasing sensitivity. The Denver and Brisbane examples show that adding a question mark changed which business ranked first and how the rest of the list was distributed. For local businesses, that means visibility is not just about being “in the market”; it also depends on how the question is framed.

The city-to-city differences are equally notable. Denver leaned toward **Alpine Maids** and **MaidPro Denver**; Austin centered on **Eleven Friends of Grumpy Brush** and **Sparkly Maid Austin Cleaning Services**; Brisbane split between **Designer Image** and **Human Nature Landscapes** in one formulation, then **Aboveground Landscapes** and **Human Nature Landscapes** in another; Sydney settled on **NBG Landscapes**.

## What this means if you’re a local business

If an AI search engine is part of the customer journey, your business needs to be easy for systems to parse, trust, and cite. That means clear entity signals across your website, consistent business details across directories, and service pages that answer common local questions in plain language.

Businesses also need content that can be lifted into a direct answer. AI systems tend to favor names they can confidently associate with a place, a service, and a relevant query. Strong crawler access, structured service information, and third-party mentions all help reduce ambiguity.

The practical takeaway is simple: local visibility is no longer only about map packs and organic rankings. It is also about whether an AI system can confidently recommend your business when someone asks a local service question.
