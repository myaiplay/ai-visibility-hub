# What is AI visibility? A plain-English guide for local businesses

<p class="meta">16 August 2026 · AI Visibility Index</p>

<div class="answer">
<strong>Direct answer:</strong> AI visibility is the likelihood that an AI search engine names your business when a customer asks for a recommendation in your category. It's measured as a rate — out of many real buyer questions ("best plumber in Denver"), in how many answers does the AI mention you? Unlike a Google ranking, there's no fixed position: answers are generated fresh each time, so visibility is a pattern across many runs, not a single slot.
</div>

For twenty years, "being found online" meant ranking on a results page. AI search replaces the results page with a sentence. When someone asks ChatGPT "who should I hire to fix a burst pipe in Denver?", the model names a handful of businesses — and everyone else simply doesn't exist in that conversation.

## How AI visibility is measured

The honest way to measure it:

1. **Write real buyer questions** for your category and city — 15–25 of them, the way customers actually ask.
2. **Run each one multiple times** per engine (ChatGPT, Google AI, Perplexity), because answers vary run to run.
3. **Score the pattern**: in what share of answers were you named? Who was named instead? What sources were cited?

The output is an *AI visibility rate* — "named in 2 of 20 relevant searches" — plus the competitor set the AI prefers, and the sources it trusts. A single screenshot of one prompt proves nothing either way.

## How it differs from SEO

| | SEO | AI visibility |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Ranked list of links | Generated sentence naming options |
| Positions | Stable, tracked daily | Regenerated every run |
| Index | Google | Google + Bing + engine-specific retrieval |
| Fixes | Keywords, links, technical | Crawler access, entity signals, citable answers |
| Measurement | Rank trackers | Repeated prompt testing |

Good SEO helps — Google's AI features use Google's index — but it's not sufficient. ChatGPT search leans on Bing; Perplexity retrieves from its own stack. Plenty of #1-ranked businesses are absent from AI answers, and some lower-ranked ones get named because their entity signals and answer-first pages are cleaner.

## The three levers

Everything that moves AI visibility falls into three buckets:

- **Technical readiness** — can the crawlers (GPTBot, PerplexityBot, Bingbot…) actually fetch your pages? See our [robots.txt allow-list guide](/articles/ai-crawler-robots-txt-guide/).
- **Entity strength** — can the model confirm you're a specific, real business? Consistent name/address/phone, LocalBusiness schema, Google Business Profile, directory listings.
- **Answer quality** — does any page give the model a short, sourced, extractable answer? Lead with 75–150 words of direct answer, then evidence.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I pay to appear in AI answers?

Not directly. There's no ad slot inside a ChatGPT or Perplexity recommendation. What you can do is remove the blockers and supply better, more citable material than competitors.

### How long does it take to improve?

Technical fixes (robots.txt, schema, indexation) can show effects within weeks as engines recrawl. Entity-building (directories, reviews, citations) compounds over months. It's a flywheel, not a switch.

### Is AI visibility just a fad?

AI answers are increasingly the first step in local purchase journeys — Google's own AI Overviews now sit above the classic results page. Whether the share is 10% or 40% for your category today, the direction is one-way, and the fixes overlap heavily with good web practice anyway.
