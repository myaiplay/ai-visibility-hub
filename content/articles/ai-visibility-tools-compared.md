## Route 1: Free DIY (start here, no matter what)

The manual version is genuinely useful:

1. Ask ChatGPT, Google AI, and Perplexity: *"best [category] in [city]"* and *"who should I hire for [service] near [city]?"* — note who gets named.
2. Open `yoursite.com/robots.txt` — confirm GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot are allowed ([copy-paste config](/articles/ai-crawler-robots-txt-guide/)).
3. Search `site:yourdomain.com` on Bing — [why that matters](/articles/chatgpt-runs-on-bing/).
4. Run our free tools: [crawler check](https://aicantfindme.com/services/ai-crawler-check?utm_source=hub&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=article_tools_compared), [readiness score](https://aicantfindme.com/services/ai-readiness?utm_source=hub&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=article_tools_compared), [index check](https://aicantfindme.com/services/index-check?utm_source=hub&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=article_tools_compared).

**Limit:** one-off prompts mislead. AI answers vary between runs — a single screenshot can show you absent when you're named 40% of the time, or vice versa. Systematic testing needs repetition.

## Route 3: Subscription monitoring (Otterly, Profound, et al.)

Continuous prompt tracking, mention counts, sentiment, competitor trends, dashboards and alerts. The category is young; pricing typically runs from tens to hundreds per month depending on prompt volume and seats — check each vendor's current page.

**Best for:** agencies managing multiple clients; brands in competitive national categories; teams running active GEO campaigns who need weekly movement data.

**Limit:** dashboards show the score; they don't fix it. A monitoring subscription on top of unfixed structural blockers is paying monthly to watch yourself lose.

## The decision in one flow

1. Do the free check. Named consistently? You may need nothing else yet.
2. Invisible or patchy? Fix the structural blockers — a one-time diagnostic compresses that discovery from days to minutes.
3. Fixed the foundations and competing in a category where visibility shifts? *Now* monitoring has something worth trending.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are free "AI visibility score" widgets accurate?

They check real things (crawler access, schema) but a score without repeated live prompt testing is a crawl audit wearing an AI costume. Useful as a technical check; not evidence of what AI actually says about you.

### Why not just track mentions in a spreadsheet myself?

You absolutely can — set 10 buyer questions, re-ask them monthly, log who gets named. It's the same method, minus automation and evidence storage. If you have the discipline, it's a legitimate free route.

### What should I ask any vendor before paying?

"Show me a sample report with real test evidence" and "how many times do you run each prompt?" Vendors with real methodology answer both immediately.
