About

AI Visibility Index is an independent research hub measuring how AI search engines — ChatGPT, Google AI, and Perplexity — recommend local businesses.

Why this exists

AI answers are becoming the front door to local commerce. But unlike Google rankings, there is no public Search Console for AI recommendations. Business owners can't see when they're skipped, who was named instead, or why. This hub exists to make that visible — with methods published openly.

Method

  • Fixed sets of real buyer-intent prompts ("best [category] in [city]", "who should I hire for [job] near [place]")
  • Run across multiple AI engines, repeated to account for variance
  • Every claim on this site traces to a recorded result or a check you can run yourself
  • We do not publish invented statistics. If a number appears here, the underlying test data does too.
