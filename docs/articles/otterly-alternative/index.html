When monitoring is the right call

  • You're actively running campaigns and need to see movement week to week
  • An agency reporting to clients, where dashboards and trend lines are the deliverable
  • A competitive national category where visibility genuinely shifts monthly

When a one-time diagnostic is the right call

  • You're a local or service business and the honest answer to "does AI recommend me?" is already "no"
  • You want to know the specific blockers — crawler access, entity gaps, missing citable answers — in priority order
  • You'd rather hand a 12-step fix plan to your web person than learn another dashboard
  • You don't want a $X9/month subscription to tell you what a single test already can

The honest overlap problem

Here's what the subscription landing pages rarely say: AI visibility blockers are mostly structural. Blocked crawlers, weak entity signals, no answer-first content. Once you've identified and fixed them, checking the same diagnosis every month adds little — the fixes take weeks to bed in regardless. Monitoring shines after the foundations are fixed and you're competing on movement. Diagnosis comes first.
